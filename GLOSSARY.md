# I Walk Everyday

An app that simulates walks and saves chosen step counts as manual entries in
Health Connect.

## Language

**Step goal**: The whole number of steps chosen for a walk.
_Avoid_: Distance, sensor count.

**Preset goal**: One of the app's predefined step goals.
_Avoid_: Template walk.

**Custom goal**: A user-entered step goal retained for reuse.
_Avoid_: Custom pace.

**Walk session**: One simulated walk from starting through its terminal outcome.
_Avoid_: Workout, recording.

**Simulated progress**: The estimated portion of a walk that has elapsed; these
steps have not yet been saved.
_Avoid_: Saved steps, measured steps.

**Saved walk**: A walk whose chosen steps were successfully written to Health
Connect as a manual entry.
_Avoid_: Finished simulation.
